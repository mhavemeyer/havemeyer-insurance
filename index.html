Who this is for

Written for the people who run fleets

Most of what I share is aimed at trucking company owners and executives, and at the owners of smaller fleets, roughly 4 to 25 power units, who often handle safety, compliance and insurance themselves alongside everything else.

If you're trying to understand why your insurance costs what it does, or what your safety data says about your operation, you're in the right place.
