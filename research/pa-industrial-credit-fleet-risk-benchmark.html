2027 Pennsylvania Industrial Credit and Fleet Risk Benchmark

Ongoing research by Michael Havemeyer, TRS, TCS · 2027 edition

An ongoing research project on how Pennsylvania manufacturers handle their trucks, plant safety, insurance, contracts and customer credit.

What it is

The 2027 Pennsylvania Industrial Credit and Fleet Risk Benchmark is a survey of manufacturers within about 100 miles of Lancaster, Pennsylvania. The featured segment for 2027 is wood products, but manufacturers in food and beverage, metal products, chemicals and plastics, building materials, paper and packaging and other industries are included. It is written for owners, CEOs, CFOs and COOs, and takes about 10 minutes. Questions are based on public research from the American Transportation Research Institute (ATRI) and Allianz Trade.

Why fleet risk and credit risk belong in the same study

For a company that runs its own trucks, the fleet, the plant, the contracts and the receivables are all part of one risk picture. A truck accident claim, a customer's demand for higher limits or additional insured status, and a slow-paying customer can each hit the same balance sheet. The benchmark looks at those risks side by side.

What the survey covers

  • Your trucks and drivers: fleet size, how product gets to customers, owned vs. leased equipment, driver turnover, and how often CSA safety scores are checked.
  • Insurance and claims: commercial auto cost changes over recent renewals, total liability limits, large claims, and workers' comp experience mod.
  • Plant, property and products: OSHA inspections, fire sprinkler coverage, and product claims or recalls.
  • Contracts and certificates: customer requests for indemnity, additional insured status or higher limits, and how outside carriers' and contractors' insurance is checked.
  • Customer credit and cash: days-to-pay, customer concentration and how it is protected, exposure to construction customers, and bad-debt write-offs.
  • Wood products: load securement, combustible dust, and stock value swings.

Your answers stay private

Results are reported only as group totals. No company or individual is ever named. Contact details are kept apart from answers and used only to send results.

What participants receive

Participating companies can receive a private, confidential company profile and scorecard that shows how they compare with their Pennsylvania peers on fleet, plant, contract and credit risk. No purchase required.
