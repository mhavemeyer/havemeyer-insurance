Uneven Enforcement Is an Insurance Issue

By Michael Havemeyer, TRS, TCS · LinkedIn post, about Oct. 29, 2026

Every time an unsafe carrier stays on the road, every responsible fleet pays.

The trucking industry's biggest problem may not be freight demand. It may be uneven enforcement.

New ATA Chairman Derek Leathers says responsible carriers shouldn't have to compete against operators that ignore safety rules and regulatory requirements. He is pushing for stronger enforcement, cargo theft legislation, lawsuit reform, and industry alignment around common standards (as reported by Transport Topics).

That's not just an ATA issue.

It's an insurance issue.
It's a shipper issue.
It's a profitability issue.

Every time an unsafe carrier stays on the road, every responsible fleet pays through higher premiums, increased scrutiny, and a damaged industry reputation.

The fleets that invest in safety, compliance, driver quality, and technology should win more business.

The question is whether regulators and policymakers will finally create the level playing field needed to make that happen. Does trucking need more enforcement, or just better enforcement of the rules already on the books?
