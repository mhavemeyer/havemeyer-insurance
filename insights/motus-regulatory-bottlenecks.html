What Does a Regulatory Bottleneck Cost a Fleet?

By Michael Havemeyer, TRS, TCS · LinkedIn post, about Oct. 29, 2026

FMCSA says Motus fixes are still “weeks away.” Here's why registration delays are a business risk, not just paperwork.

FMCSA Deputy Administrator Jesse Elison acknowledged that the agency's troubled Motus registration platform is still preventing some legitimate carriers from making registration changes. While FMCSA says improvements are coming in "weeks, not months," there is still no firm timeline for a full resolution (as reported by Yahoo and FreightWaves).

Why this matters

  • Carriers needing authority changes, updates, or registration modifications may continue experiencing delays.
  • FMCSA admits the system was built to stop fraud, but legitimate operators are getting caught in the process.
  • Administrative delays can affect onboarding new customers, adding equipment, acquisitions, mergers, and growth initiatives.

The bigger question: What is the cost to a fleet when a regulatory bottleneck slows down revenue-producing activity?

The carriers that navigate these disruptions best tend to have strong compliance processes, documentation controls, and contingency plans in place before a change becomes urgent.
